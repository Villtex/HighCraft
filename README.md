# HighCraft - Drug Mod for NeoForge 1.21.1

Grow, process and trade: five crops with volumetric 3D models, LSD from ergot, meth and ecstasy from your own drug lab,
twelve machines and devices, lab furniture and protective gear, drug labs in villages,
two villager professions, a quality, addiction and tolerance system, and screen effects.
Supports JEI and is compatible with Sodium and Iris.

| | |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| NeoForge | 21.1.x (tested: 21.1.265) |
| Java | 21 |
| Optional | JEI 19.x, Sodium (tested: 0.6.13 and 0.8.x), Iris (tested: 1.8.12 + Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3) |
| Add-on | **HighCraft: Create** (separate jar) - requires Create 6.0.x (tested: 6.0.10) |

## Building

```bash
./gradlew build
```

Output in `build/libs/`:
* `highcraft-neoforge-1.21.1-1.2.0.jar` - the mod
* `highcraft_create-neoforge-1.21.1-1.2.0.jar` - the Create add-on (only use it together with Create and HighCraft)

Testing in the dev environment (`-Pdevtest` additionally runs an automated screenshot scenario):

```bash
./gradlew runClient                              # normal client (with JEI)
./gradlew runClient -Puse_sodium_in_dev=true     # also with Sodium
./gradlew runClient -Puse_iris_in_dev=true       # with Sodium + Iris (put a shader pack into run/shaderpacks)
./gradlew runClient -Puse_create_in_dev=true     # with Create 6.0.10 + add-on
./gradlew runServer                              # dedicated server
```

The assets (textures, models, blockstates, language file, recipes, loot tables, worldgen) are generated by the
Python scripts in `tools/`:

```bash
python3 tools/gen_assets.py && python3 tools/gen_lang.py && python3 tools/gen_data.py && python3 tools/gen_addon.py
```

Requires Python 3 with Pillow and NumPy. The 32x32 item icons come from `tools/tex_items_hd.py`, which is built on a small
SDF pixel-art renderer (`tools/hd.py`: shapes as signed distance fields, a fixed top-left light, hue-shifted colour ramps
and selective outlines).

## Content

### Crops (volumetric 3D models per growth stage)
The plants are made of real geometry: stems and angled side branches as cuboids, tilted fan leaves on several levels,
3D buds, poppy flowers and pods, and coca as a voxel shrub with berries.

| Crop | Soil | Special |
|---|---|---|
| **Cannabis** | farmland | grows two blocks tall from stage 4, 3D colas, right-click harvest with regrowth |
| **Tobacco** | farmland | large tilted leaves, pink flowers, right-click harvest |
| **Opium poppy** | farmland | red 3D flowers -> pods; scoring them with the **scoring knife** yields raw opium |
| **Coca bush** | dirt, grass, farmland | perennial, red berries, grows faster in warm biomes |
| **Magic mushrooms** | dark, or mycelium/podzol | 3D mushroom clusters, glow faintly, spread |

Wild plants spawn in suitable biomes. Seeds also drop from grass and are sold by the wandering trader and the villagers.

**Quality (1-5 ★):** +1 fertilized, +1 plenty of light (sun or grow lamp), +1 wet farmland or a suitable climate,
+1 with 30 % luck. Quality is carried through all machines and crafting recipes and extends the effect by up to 60 %.

### LSD
* **Ergot** sometimes infests mature wheat (about 5 % when harvesting) and is rarely sold by the wandering trader and the dealer.
* Chemistry lab: 2x ergot + lab reagent + solvent -> **LSD solution**; with paper -> 6x **LSD blotter**
* **Acid trip** effect: kaleidoscope at the screen edges, neon edges, colour cycles, a "breathing" world, strong camera drift, hallucinations.
  It lasts very long and is barely addictive, but tolerance builds quickly.

### Meth, ecstasy & drug labs
All chemistry is fictional game chemistry with invented ingredients.

| Step | Machine | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Chemical precursor | Chemistry lab | 2 sugar + gunpowder + lab reagent -> 2 precursor |
| Aromatic oil | Extractor | 2 spruce logs + solvent |
| Catalyst pellets | Crafting | iron nugget + redstone + glowstone dust -> 4 |
| Raw meth batch | **Chemical reactor** | 2 precursor + catalyst + solvent |
| Raw MDMA batch | **Chemical reactor** | 2 aromatic oil + catalyst + reagent |
| Crystal meth / MDMA crystals | **Crystallizer** | batch -> 4 crystals |
| MDMA powder | Herb grinder | crystals -> 2 powder |
| Ecstasy pills | **Pill press** | powder + sugar + dye -> 4 pills (pink heart, blue star, yellow smiley, purple diamond) |

* **Chemical reactor:** heats three reagents (fuel or FE). It needs a **fume hood** or **exhaust fan** within 4 blocks. Without
  ventilation it releases **toxic fumes** (poison + nausea for everyone nearby without a gas mask), a batch can boil over and explode
  (no block damage; both can be turned off in the config) and the quality drops by 1; with ventilation it rises by 1.
* **Crystallizer:** needs cold: ice, packed ice, blue ice, snow or powder snow in the coolant slot, or FE. Packed or blue ice next to it
  ("deep cold") runs it for free at full speed and adds +1 quality; a cold biome or plain ice runs it at half speed.
* **Pill press:** powder + sugar as binder + a dye.
* **Effects:** *Meth High* (fast, tireless, strong mining speed, no hunger, can't sleep, racing heart, twitchy over-sharpened cold vision;
  extremely addictive) and *Euphoria* (ecstasy: warm pink bloom, the world pulses to the beat, hearts above everyone, slow healing but
  more hunger). Both end in a *Comedown*. Crystal meth can be snorted or smoked in a **glass pipe** (stronger).
* **Protective gear:** **gas mask** (no fume damage) and the **hazmat suit, pants and boots**; the full set also makes you immune to lab accidents.
* **Lab blocks:** fume hood, exhaust fan, lab tiles (+ slab, stairs), hazard stripes, lab counter, chemical barrel, hazard barrel,
  gas cylinder, glassware shelf, plastic sheeting, toxic hazard sign and lab ceiling light.
* **Village drug labs:** plains, desert, savanna, snowy and taiga villages sometimes contain a lab house in their own building style
  with working machines, a fume hood, a crystallizer on packed ice, a loot chest and a precision scale (villagers can become dealers).
  Frequency: config `villageLabWeight` (default 2; vanilla houses use 1-5, 0 turns village labs off).

### Item icons & 3D item models
All 2D items (including the add-on's intermediate items) have hand-designed **32x32 icons** with consistent lighting,
hue-shifted shading and outlines.
Joint, spliff, cigarette, cigar, pipe, lighter, syringes, cannabis buds, magic mushrooms, poppy pod, hashish, LSD blotter,
solvent canister and tea cups also have 3D models for the hand, ground, item frames and the drying rack
(`neoforge:separate_transforms`). The inventory keeps the crisp 2D icon.

### Machines & devices
| Block | Function |
|---|---|
| **Drying rack** | hang up to 4 parts (visible, gently swinging); speed depends on climate, sun and rain; works with hoppers |
| **Herb grinder** | buds -> ground cannabis, tobacco -> cut tobacco, poppy pods -> seeds ... |
| **Extractor** | with solvent: resin, coca paste, raw opium |
| **Chemistry lab** | 3 inputs: cocaine, morphine, LSD |
| **Hydraulic press** | resin -> hashish, hemp fiber -> paper |
| **Grow tent** | weather-independent growing with water, fertilizer and light (power, lamp or sun); the plant visibly grows inside |
| **Grow lamp** | light level 15, speeds up crops up to 5 blocks below |
| **Bong** | fill with water, pack, smoke |
| **Precision scale** | the dealer's workstation, shows weight and quality |
| **Chemical reactor** | 3 reagents + heat; needs ventilation (see above) |
| **Crystallizer** | crystallizes raw batches; needs cold |
| **Pill press** | powder + binder + dye -> pills |
| **Fume hood / exhaust fan** | ventilate reactors within 4 blocks |

All GUI machines run on **fuel** (like a furnace) or **FE power** and support hoppers/pipes and comparators.

### Consumption, effects & addiction
* Effects: *Stoned, Munchies, Cocaine Rush -> Crash, Sedated, Psychedelic Trip, Acid Trip, Nicotine Buzz, Meth High, Euphoria -> Comedown, Toxic Fumes, Withdrawal, Overdose*
* Every substance has its own addiction, tolerance and craving values. Tolerance shortens the effect, addiction leads to withdrawal.
* An intoxication level above 100 means overdose, above 170 it is lethal. Antidote: detox tea.
* Smokables need a **lighter** (or flint and steel).
* Visuals: full-screen shaders (waves, colour shifts, blur, chromatic aberration, vignettes), camera sway,
  FOV pulsing, hallucination particles and sounds, smoke puffs. Everything can be turned off in the client config.
* `/highcraft status`, `/highcraft reset`, `/highcraft addiction <player> <type> <value>`

### Villagers
* **Dealer** (workstation: precision scale) buys finished goods and sells accessories, chemicals and rare seeds.
* **Grower** (workstation: drying rack) buys harvests and sells seeds, fertilizer, grow lamps and grow tents.

### Add-on "HighCraft: Create" (`highcraft_create`)
Automate the whole processing chain with Create machines (49 recipes, all visible in Create's JEI categories):

| Create machine | Recipes |
|---|---|
| Millstone / crushing wheels | buds -> ground cannabis, tobacco -> cut tobacco, poppy pods -> seeds (+ raw opium), cannabis leaves -> fiber (+ resin) |
| Mixer (basin) | resin, coca paste, cocaine (superheated), morphine, LSD solution (superheated), raw opium, solvent (with water), reagent, fertilizer, coca/mushroom/detox tea (with water, heated), space cookies (with Create chocolate) |
| Compacting (basin + press) | 2 resin -> hashish, 3 hemp fiber -> 3 paper, MDMA powder + sugar + dye -> 4 ecstasy pills |
| Mixer (meth/ecstasy) | precursor (heated), raw meth batch (superheated), raw MDMA batch (heated) |
| Millstone / crushing wheels (MDMA) | MDMA crystals -> MDMA powder |
| Fan + fire (smoking) | drying of all plant parts (also works in the smoker) |
| Fan + soul fire (haunting) | wheat -> **ergot**, poppy -> poppy seeds, glow lichen -> mushroom spores |
| Deployer | morphine + empty syringe -> morphine syringe; scoring knife on poppy pod -> raw opium (the knife is kept) |
| Sequenced assembly | joint, spliff, 2 cigarettes, cigar (3 loops), 8 LSD blotters (with a saw), 3 syringes - with intermediate items and progress bars |

Teas, vials and solvent also stand upright on the belt, and the mechanical harvester cleanly harvests the two-block cannabis plant
(the plant regrows just like after a right-click harvest). Note: processing with Create does not keep the quality stars.

### Compatibility
* **Sodium:** only standard JSON models (render types via `render_type`), block entity renderers and the vanilla PostChain.
  There are no mixins into the chunk pipeline.
* **Iris:** the drug shaders are applied as the lowest HUD layer, i.e. after the shader pack has written its final image.
  They therefore also work with an active shader pack and include the hand. Whether a shader pack is active is detected automatically
  through the Iris API (without a hard dependency). The client option `shadersWithShaderpack` turns the screen shaders off while a
  shader pack is active; the HUD vignette then stays on as a fallback. Tested with Complementary Reimagined: plants, machines, glass and
  particles render correctly and cast shadows.
* **JEI:** own categories for all machines including the grow tent, catalysts, clickable progress arrow, "+" recipe transfer and info pages.
* **Large modpacks:** tested in a 200+ mod Fantasy pack (ModernFix, More Culling, Sinytra Connector, Sable/Veil, FancyMenu, Sodium 0.8, Iris 1.8).

## Changelog

### 1.2.0
* New: **meth** and **ecstasy** with their own production chain (fictional in-game chemistry), three new machines (chemical reactor,
  crystallizer, pill press), ventilation (fume hood, exhaust fan), toxic fumes and lab accidents, glass pipe and four pill designs.
* New: protective gear (gas mask, hazmat suit, pants, boots) and 13 lab building blocks for building complete labs.
* New: drug labs generate in plains, desert, savanna, snowy and taiga villages (config `villageLabWeight`).
* New effects: Meth High, Euphoria, Comedown, Toxic Fumes - with their own shader, camera, sound and particle effects.
* Create add-on: 9 more recipes for the new chain.

### 1.1.0
* All 52 item icons redrawn at **32x32** (previously 16x16): consistent top-left lighting, hue-shifted colour ramps, selective outlines,
  new designs (e.g. resin on parchment, seeds in a wooden bowl, opium wrapped in a poppy petal, ergot on a wheat ear, upright drug test).
* The 3D LSD blotter and solvent canister now use their own high-resolution face textures.
* The mod is now **English only**: language file, config descriptions, mod descriptions, log messages, source comments, tools and README.

### 1.0.1
* Fix: in large modpacks (e.g. with **More Culling**) the client got stuck on the loading screen / showed a black screen instead of the
  title menu. Cause: the cannabis plant returned an invalid hitbox for a block state that never occurs in game (upper half, age < 4);
  More Culling queries every state while loading. All shapes are now precomputed and clamped.
